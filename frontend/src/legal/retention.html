The windows are set under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and its Rules, 2025: personal data is kept only for the purpose it was collected for, and then erased or anonymised, unless another Indian law requires us to keep it longer.

The rules behind these windows

  • Erase when the purpose is served. Section 8(7) of the DPDP Act requires erasure when you withdraw consent or when it is reasonable to assume the purpose is no longer served, whichever is earlier, unless a law requires us to retain it. We also make sure our service providers erase the data they hold for us.
  • Legal retention overrides. Where another law requires a longer period (tax, company law, telecom or cyber-security directions), we keep only the records that law covers, and only for that period.
  • Minimum log period. Rule 8(3) of the DPDP Rules, 2025 sets a minimum of one year for personal data, traffic data and processing logs in the cases listed in its Seventh Schedule. We apply a 12-month log window across the Site as a precaution.
  • Notice before erasure. The three-year inactivity erasure and 48-hour advance notice in Rule 8 and the Third Schedule apply to e-commerce, online gaming and social media platforms above set user numbers. They do not currently apply to us, and our windows are shorter than three years.
  • Dates. The DPDP Rules were notified on 13 November 2025, and most of their obligations take effect on 13 May 2027. We follow these windows from now.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or erase your personal data, or withdraw consent, at any time by emailing care@nimantran.ai. We act on a request without waiting for the window to end, unless a law requires us to keep the record. If you are not satisfied, use the grievance process in our Privacy Policy and then, if needed, the Data Protection Board of India.

These windows are reviewed at least once a year and whenever the law or our products change. The date at the top shows the latest update.
