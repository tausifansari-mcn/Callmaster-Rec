This Site uses very little in the way of cookies or trackers. Here's exactly what runs, in plain terms.

1. Strictly necessary

The Site enforces the one-trial-per-phone-number rule server-side (a database check on each sandbox), not via a cookie. No cookie is required for this to work.

2. Fonts

The Site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. This involves a request to Google's font-hosting servers when the page loads; Google's own privacy policy governs that request. No advertising or tracking cookie is set by this.

3. Analytics and advertising

This Site does not currently run analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics are added, this page will be updated first to disclose what's added and why, along with any consent banner required.

4. Changes to this policy

We'll update this page before adding any new cookie, tag or tracking technology to the Site.
