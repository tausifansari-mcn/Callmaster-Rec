This policy explains what personal data this Site collects, why, and what happens to it. We don't claim to store no data — where we do collect it, this page says so plainly, and every sandbox step that collects personal data is gated behind an explicit consent checkbox before you can continue.

1. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, email address and phone number, entered on the Contact page or at the start of a sandbox trial.
  • Sandbox content: for Quality Audits — the call recording you upload, and, if you choose, your own quality sheet or manually entered scoring parameters; for Voice Bots — the script you upload and the phone number to be called.
  • Trial-verification data: the OTP verification status tied to the phone number you enter, used only to confirm the number and enforce the one-trial-per-number limit.
  • Generated outputs: the transcript and scorecard produced for a Quality Audits trial, or the call outcome for a Voice Bots trial.

2. Why we collect it

Purely to run the trial you asked for — verifying your number, producing your transcript and scorecard or placing your demo call — and, where you've used the Contact page, to respond to your enquiry. We don't use sandbox uploads for advertising.

3. Legal basis and consent

Each sandbox shows a consent screen before any personal data is processed, listing what is collected and why. Progress is blocked until you check the consent box. You may withdraw consent for future use by not proceeding, and can request deletion of what's already been submitted (see Your rights, below).

4. How long we keep it

Retention periods differ by category (contact details, sandbox content, generated outputs) and are being finalized internally. See the Data Retention Policy for the category-level breakdown, and this page for the confirmed windows once published.

5. Who we share it with

Data is processed by the Nimantran team and by the service providers needed to run a given trial — for example, a telephony provider to place the Voice Bots demo call. We don't sell personal data, and we don't share sandbox uploads with third parties for purposes unrelated to delivering your trial.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

  • Right to access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction and updating of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure, once it is no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, as easily as it was given.
  • Right to grievance redressal through our Grievance Officer (below) or the Data Protection Board of India.
  • Right to nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

7. Grievance Officer

Our Grievance Officer is Rajesh Ramachandran. Write to rajesh.ramachandran@teammas.in. General privacy requests can also go to care@nimantran.ai.

8. Security

We apply access controls and encryption in transit to data submitted through the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's data

The Site and its sandboxes are intended for business use by adults evaluating Nimantran's products. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as data practices are finalized. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.
