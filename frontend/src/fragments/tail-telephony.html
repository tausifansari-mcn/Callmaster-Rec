Cloud Telephony: frequently asked questions

What is a mobile look-alike number?

A calling number in a mobile-style series, so the call looks like it is coming from a person and not from a call center.

No. Agents need a laptop or phone and an internet connection.

Can I port my existing number?

Number porting depends on your current provider and number type. Tell us what you have and we will confirm what is possible.
