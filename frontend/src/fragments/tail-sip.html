SIP Channels: frequently asked questions

What is a concurrent call channel?

One channel carries one call at a time. If 40 customers can be on the phone at once, you need 40 channels.

How do I know how many I need?

Divide peak-hour call minutes by 60 and add a safety margin. Or give us your volume and we will size it.

Does it work with my dialer or PBX?

Most modern dialers and PBX systems support SIP. Share your setup and we will confirm compatibility.

What uptime can I expect?

Capacity is built to a contact-center standard. Specific service levels are agreed in your contract.

How is it priced?

By channel count and volume, quoted after you tell us what you need.

Can I add channels later?

Yes. Capacity can be increased, and reduced, as your campaigns change.

Get capacity that matches your peak, not your average.
