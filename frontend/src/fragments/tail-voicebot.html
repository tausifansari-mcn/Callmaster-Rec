Voice Bots: frequently asked questions

Will customers know it is a bot?

Our voices are natural, and we recommend identifying the call as automated. Honest framing builds more trust than pretending.

Which languages and accents?

English, Hindi and Hinglish with Indian, British and American accents. Regional languages can be scoped on request.

Can I try one before buying?

Yes. Enter your script, choose the voice and Nimantran calls your phone. Each phone number gets one free trial.

What can a voice bot handle?

Collections reminders, appointment confirmations, abandoned-cart recovery, order and delivery updates, feedback calls and first-level customer service.

How much does it cost?

From ₹3.5 per minute. Tell us your volume and we will quote it, and see whether we can match your current rate.

Does it follow calling rules and DND?

Outbound calling is subject to telecom rules. We design campaigns around consented and permitted contacts, and you remain responsible for the consent of the numbers you upload.

Hear one on your own phone, or book a meeting and we will script your first campaign with you.
