Questions buyers ask us

Who is behind Nimantran?

Nimantran is built by the team at Mas Callnet, a contact center and BPO company that has run customer conversations for more than 23 years.

Do I need to replace my current systems?

No. Nimantran is designed to plug into how your team already works. You can start with one product, such as call audits, and add others later.

Can I use my own scorecard and script?

Yes. Quality Audits can score against a standard scorecard or your own parameters, and voice bots run the script you write.

Which languages do the voice bots speak?

English, Hindi and Hinglish, with regional languages available. Test the exact language mix your customers use during the free trial.

How is my data handled?

Contact is only with your consent, which we ask for each time you enter your details. Retention windows for each type of data are published on the Data Retention Policy page.
