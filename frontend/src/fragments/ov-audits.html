What Quality Audits does

Quality Audits listens to a call recording the way a senior QA analyst would: it transcribes the conversation, scores it against the parameters you care about, estimates how satisfied the customer was, and tells you which calls need coaching or urgent attention. The difference is that it does this for every call, not a 2% sample.

How it works

  1. Upload a recordingDrop in one of your own calls. No integration needed to try it.
  2. Pick the parametersChoose the standard scorecard or describe your own.
  3. Read the resultGet the transcript, the score and the coaching points in minutes.

Highlights

Every call scored

Replace sampling with full coverage, so a bad call is found when it happens, not at the next quarterly review.

Your own scorecard

Use standard parameters or define your own: greeting, empathy, compliance lines, product knowledge, closing.

Transcript you can search

Each recording becomes a readable transcript, so you can find what was promised and when.

Coaching flags

Calls that miss a parameter are flagged with the reason, so team leads coach on evidence instead of hunches.

Works across call types

Inbound service, outbound sales, collections and campaign calls, each with its own scoring logic.

Compliance evidence

Show that mandatory disclosures were read out, call by call, whenever an auditor or client asks.

Perceived NPS and CSAT

Get an estimated satisfaction and promoter score for every call, read from what the customer said and how, with no survey needed.

Fraud alerts

Calls with signs of fraud, such as social engineering, identity or OTP requests, or suspicious account changes, are flagged for review.

Social media escalation alerts

Calls where the customer threatens to post online, go to the media or to a regulator are flagged, so the case is handled before it spreads.
